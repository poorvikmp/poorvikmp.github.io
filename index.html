About

Curious about what's real in markets

I'm drawn to the question underneath every backtest: is this result real, or did I just find a pattern in noise? My first project started as a market-timing idea and turned into a lesson in out-of-sample testing and finding where returns really come from. My second asked when the stock-bond hedge breaks down, and found that the answer is a risk to manage rather than a trade.

I work mostly in Python, and I'm looking for opportunities in quantitative research, portfolio analytics and investment strategy.

How I work

  1. Tune on one period, judge on another. The test data stays untouched until the end.
  2. Beat the simple alternative. A strategy has to beat what you could do with no signals at all.
  3. Report what didn't work. The failures explain the result as much as the wins.
